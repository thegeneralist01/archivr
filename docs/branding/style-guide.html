01
The Logo
The Archivr logo consists of two elements: the mark — a capital A in Cormorant Garamond set inside a rounded square in Terracotta — and the wordmark. Use both together as the primary logo. Never separate them in contexts where the brand is being introduced for the first time.

05
Color
The palette is organized by role. Shell and Paper form the light/dark surface pair. Terracotta is the single brand accent — use it with restraint. Amber is a secondary accent for highlights and metadata. Never introduce colors outside this palette in brand contexts.

Typography
Three typefaces cover every context. Cormorant Garamond is the brand voice — it appears in the logo, headings, and display text. Helvetica Neue handles all interface chrome. Spectral carries long-form reading content.
